The Profile

"She carries swimwear the way editorial models carry couture — long lines, clean angles, and a face that reads warm on camera."

Sammie is a swimwear and lifestyle model with a lean, athletic frame, platinum styling, and signature fine-line tattoos that give commercial work an editorial edge. Across 85+ published sets she's shown a consistent, professional command of posing — she knows her angles cold, moves cleanly through a shoot, and translates a wardrobe change into a genuine mood change.

Her strongest territory is sun-and-water brand storytelling: swim, resort, and beachside lifestyle that feels aspirational but reachable. Paired with her automotive and intimates sets, she reads as a model who can anchor a seasonal swim campaign and still deliver a moody editorial on the same card.
